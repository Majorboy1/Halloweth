# Guitar samples

The nylon-string and steel-string recordings are from Meta Open Sound:

- https://github.com/facebookincubator/Meta-open-sound/tree/main/systems/instruments/meta-nylon-guitar
- https://github.com/facebookincubator/Meta-open-sound/tree/main/systems/instruments/meta-steel-guitar

Both source collections are released under CC0 1.0 Universal. Their license texts
are included with each collection. Halloweth bundles a compact subset of 15 sampled
notes and three recorded velocity layers per instrument, mapped for chromatic play.
The audio files are the original OGG previews; no synthesized replacements are used
for the factory presets named Velvet Nylon Guitar and Steel String Glow.
